Real time · Cameras only · 4–16 arms

AI teams run a factory hall of robot arms.

In real time, seeing the world only through cameras. 36 long jobs, up to 16 arms on one job, and every run is on video.

The four jobs

Jobs that no arm can finish alone.

Each job comes in three team sizes and three levels, 36 tasks in all. A bigger team has more arms and more work to divide. A higher level makes the same job physically or visually harder. Each clip is a real run, and opens it.

Transport

Deliver every item to its place on the right board. Any arm may carry any item, and items cross the table by being handed over or set down for the next arm.

Arms
4, 8, 12
Levels
Flat targets, then sockets, then sockets on a sprung board

Mosaic

Build a reference picture, one colored cube per cell. The picture has four colors, and every cell needs the right one.

Arms
4, 6, 8
Levels
Pockets, then printed lines, then unmarked cells

Relay

Carry long timbers to the goal. It takes at least two arms to carry one, and each timber must change hands in the air at least once.

Arms
8, 12, 16
Levels
Straight, then curved with a hanging weight, then hinged with a hanging weight

Dial

Find a hidden code by turning spring-loaded knobs and holding them all at once. The only hint is a display of the best try so far.

Arms
4 to 16
Levels
Knobs on the table, then modules held in the air, then cranks

How it works

Look, decide, act, while the clock runs.

  1. Step 1

    See

    The agents get pictures from fixed cameras and the state of their own arms. They never get object positions, depth or contacts.

  2. Step 2

    Decide

    One agent may control every arm, or a controller may split the job and hand arms to helpers. They can write and run their own code.

  3. Step 3

    Act

    One robot tool moves the arms: move an arm to a pose; open, close or grasp with a gripper. That is all an arm can be told.

The clock never stops

The hall is simulated in real time and does not wait while an agent thinks. A run ends when the agents say the job is done, or after 24 hours of work.

Nobody says it worked

The agents look again, and when a move or a grasp has failed they try again. Every failure stays in the record, and an evaluator they cannot reach decides whether the job is really done.

Get started

Run it, or cite it.

git clone https://github.com/changxuanfan/RoboTeam.git
cd RoboTeam
uv sync --locked
.venv/bin/roboteam bootstrap
.venv/bin/roboteam plan --task-types relay --level 1
